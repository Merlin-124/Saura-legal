Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026 · Version 1.0

This policy supplements the Saura Privacy Policy. It describes how Saura, operated by Merlin Padilla, an individual, collects, uses and shares "consumer health data" as that term is used by the Washington My Health My Data Act, the Nevada consumer health data law and similar laws. It applies to everyone who uses Saura.

1. What consumer health data we collect, and why

  • Body and fitness information you enter — body weight, body fat percentage, height, sex, activity level, training goal, workout logs (exercises, sets, reps, weights), nutrition and water logs, and progress photos you choose to upload. Purpose: to provide the features you use (tracking, trends, calorie and macro targets, your recovery view).
  • Apple Health data, only if you connect it — sleep, resting and walking heart rate, heart rate variability, heart-rate recovery, VO₂ max, breathing rate, sleeping wrist temperature, blood oxygen, steps, body weight, body fat percentage and nutrition logged in other apps. Purpose: to calculate your recovery score and show your sleep, vitals, body and nutrition trends.
  • Apple Watch workout data — heart rate and active energy during a workout you run on the Saura watch app, and the finished workout, which Saura saves to Apple Health. Purpose: to show them live and in your workout summary.

2. Where it comes from

Directly from you (what you enter or upload) and, with your permission, from Apple Health on your iPhone and Apple Watch.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell consumer health data. We do not share it with any third party or affiliate for any purpose; there are currently none. We store it with our hosting provider, Supabase, which acts only on our behalf as a processor under its service terms (see the Privacy Policy, section 4). Other Saura users see a workout only if you attach it to a post you choose to share. People who follow you and train for the same goal can see on the Goal board that you share their goal and your 0-100 training scores (consistency, progression and balance), which are worked out from your workout logs, not from Apple Health. Your recovery score, weight, height, sex, nutrition logs, Apple Health readings and progress photos are never visible to other users. We may disclose information when the law requires it.

4. Your rights

  • Confirm and access the consumer health data we hold about you, and the list of third parties it is shared with.
  • Withdraw consent at any time by turning off Saura's access in the Health app (your profile → Apps → Saura) or by deleting the data in Saura.
  • Delete your data by deleting your account in Saura (Settings → Account → Delete account) or by emailing us. We delete it from our active systems and tell our processors to delete it. Copies in backups are removed as the backups expire, and in any case within 6 months of confirming your request. What deleting an account does not remove is described in the Privacy Policy, section 5.
  • Appeal a decision about a request by replying to our response and saying you appeal. We will answer your appeal in writing within 45 days. If we deny it, we will tell you how to contact your state attorney general (in Washington: atg.wa.gov/file-complaint).

Email saura.app.help@gmail.com to make a request. We respond within 45 days. If we need more time, we may take up to 45 more days and will tell you why within the first 45 days.

5. Consent

Before Saura asks Apple for access to your Health data, it shows a short screen in the app that says what it will read, why, and that a copy of the daily values is stored with our hosting provider; you tap Continue or Not now. We keep a record of that choice (the date and time, and the version of this policy shown). Only after you continue do you see Apple's own permission screen, where you approve each data type. We will not collect or share consumer health data for any purpose beyond those described here without asking for your separate consent first.

6. Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will tell you in the app and ask you to review it before you continue.
