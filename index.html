Saura

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026 · Version 1.4

Saura ("we," "our," "us") is a fitness and social training app. We take your privacy seriously — especially because the app involves body measurements, training history, and personal photos. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and what control you have over your data.

When you create an account or sign in, Saura asks you to review and accept this Privacy Policy together with our Terms of Use, and keeps a record of what you accepted and when (see section 2). If you do not agree, please don't use the app. Residents of Washington, Nevada and other states with consumer health data laws: see our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

1. Who we are

Saura is operated by Merlin Padilla. We are the data controller for all personal data described in this policy. You can reach us at saura.app.help@gmail.com for any privacy question, request, or complaint.

2. Information we collect

Account information

  • Email address (required to sign up and sign in).
  • Password (stored only as a one-way hash by our authentication provider — we never see your plaintext password).
  • Display name and avatar (if you provide them).
  • A record of your acceptance of our Terms and this Privacy Policy: the version of each that you accepted, the date and time, how you accepted (sign-up screen or the in-app Terms & Privacy screen), the app version and the platform. We keep it as proof of your consent and to know whether you need to review an updated version. After you delete your account we keep a minimal record of when you accepted which version of the Terms, with your email address and account ID replaced by a one-way code (a salted hash), for up to five years, only to prove an agreement if there is a dispute. It contains no name, email address or account ID.
  • Date of birth (collected at sign-up to verify you meet our minimum age of 16, and also used to work out your age for the calorie and macro targets in the Nutrition tab; you can edit it in Settings). It is stored with your account record and is never shown to other users.
  • Sign in with Apple (optional). If you choose "Continue with Apple", Apple confirms your identity and gives us a unique Apple user identifier and the email address you choose to share. That may be a private relay address if you use "Hide My Email". We use these only to create your account and sign you in. You can stop using Sign in with Apple for Saura at any time in your Apple Account settings. Apple's own handling of your data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Health and body information

  • Body weight, body fat percentage, height, sex, activity level, and training goal (if you provide them during onboarding, in Settings, or in your daily log).
  • Workout logs: exercises, sets, reps, weights, dates.
  • Nutrition logs: calories, macronutrients, water intake (if you choose to log them).
  • Apple Health (HealthKit) — only if you connect it. With your permission, Saura reads: sleep (time asleep and sleep stages), resting heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), walking heart rate average, heart-rate recovery, VO₂ max, breathing (respiratory) rate, sleeping wrist temperature, blood oxygen, steps, body weight, body fat percentage, and nutrition you've logged in other apps (calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat and water). We use it only to calculate your recovery score, show your sleep, vitals, body and nutrition trends, and fill in your logs. A copy of these daily values is stored with your account on our hosting provider (Supabase) so your history works across devices, and it is deleted when you delete your account. If you use the Saura Apple Watch app, it also reads your heart rate and active energy while a workout is running, to show them live on your watch and in your workout summary, and when you finish the workout it saves that workout (a strength-training session with its duration, heart rate and calories) to Apple Health, where you can see or delete it in the Health app. Saura writes nothing else to Apple Health. The Saura card on your watch (Smart Stack or watch face) can also show your latest heart rate; that reading is read on your watch and stays on your watch. The sets, reps and weights you log on your watch are saved to your Saura account like any other workout. Saura never uses Health data for advertising or marketing. It does not sell your Health data or share it with anyone except Supabase, which stores it for us. You can turn access off at any time in the Health app → your profile → Apps → Saura.
  • Progress photos (if you choose to upload them). Progress photos are stored in a private bucket and visible only to you; they are never shown to other users or in the social feed.

Social-feature information

  • Posts you create (caption, photos or videos, attached workout).
  • Likes, comments, and follows you create (visible to other users through the normal social-feed mechanics).
  • Blocks you create (visible only to you — the blocked user is never notified).
  • Reports you file against other users, posts or comments (visible only to you and to our moderation review). We keep a copy of the reported text with the report so we can review it even if it is deleted.

Device and technical information

  • Push notification token (required to deliver notifications). Stored only while you remain signed in on that device.
  • IP address at sign-in (logged automatically by our authentication provider for security/fraud detection).
  • App usage events. To understand which features are used and improve Saura, the app records a small set of events linked to your account, such as opening the app, starting a workout, connecting Apple Health, logging food, creating a routine, viewing the upgrade screen, or viewing a weekly review. They stay in our own database, are never shared with analytics or advertising companies, and are deleted when you delete your account.
  • Error and crash reports. If the app hits an error while you are signed in, it sends a short report to our own database with your user ID, the error message and technical details (such as which part of the app failed), the app version and your device platform. We use these only to find and fix bugs. They are not shared with analytics or advertising companies and are deleted when you delete your account.

What we do NOT collect or do today

As of the date at the top of this policy:

  • We do not collect your precise location (no GPS access).
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your Apple Health data, workout logs, body measurements, nutrition logs or progress photos for advertising or marketing.
  • We do not currently show third-party advertising. See "Advertising" in section 4 about advertising we plan to add.
  • We do not use your training data to train external AI models.

3. How we use your information

We use the information above to:

  • Provide the core features of the app — log workouts, compute analytics, display recovery scores, generate your goal-aligned leaderboard, render the social feed.
  • Deliver push notifications you've enabled (likes, comments, friend PRs).
  • Personalize the experience to your training goal and preferences.
  • Understand how features are used so we can improve Saura (usage events and error reports described in section 2).
  • Detect and prevent abuse, spam, and harassment.

4. Who we share it with

Service providers (data processors)

We use trusted third parties to run the service. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, file storage, and edge functions. Stores all of the information described in section 2. Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Expo — delivers push notifications via Apple's push notification service and delivers app updates (when the app checks for an update, Expo receives standard technical data such as your IP address, device model and app version). For notifications it receives the push token and the notification payload. The payload contains the notifying user's display name and, for comments, a short sanitized preview of the comment text. Expo Privacy Policy.
  • Apple — receives the notification payload (including the notifying user's display name and, for comments, a short comment preview) to deliver pushes to your device, and, if you use it, provides Sign in with Apple. Apple's data practices are governed by their Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — manages and verifies your Saura Pro subscription. Receives your user ID and App Store purchase/ subscription data (receipt status, entitlement, renewal dates) — never your payment card details, which Apple handles directly. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Advertising (planned)

Saura does not show third-party advertising today. We plan to earn revenue from advertising in the future, for example advertisements or sponsored placements in the social feed and affiliate links to retailers such as Amazon. If you tap an affiliate link or an ad, the retailer or ad provider receives the information your device sends when you open it (such as your IP address, device type and the fact that you came from Saura) and may use its own cookies or identifiers under its own privacy policy; we do not give it your Saura account details. Before any advertising starts we will update this policy to name the advertising providers and the information they receive, and we will ask you to review and accept the update. Where Apple or the law requires your permission first (for example Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, or consent under privacy laws), we will ask before any tracking or sharing for advertising begins. We will never give advertisers or advertising providers your Apple Health data, workout logs, body measurements, nutrition logs or progress photos.

Other Saura users

If you post to the feed, follow another user, or comment on a post, your display name, avatar, and that activity are visible to other Saura users. Images and avatars are stored so that anyone who has the link to an image can open it, even without a Saura account. If you turn on "Private account" in Settings, only people you approve can follow you and see your posts, but the image files themselves can still be opened by anyone who has their exact link. When you attach a workout to a post, that workout's exercises, sets, reps, and weights are visible to anyone who can view the post.

Goal board. The Goal board shows you and the people you follow who train for the same goal as you. People who follow you and share your goal can see your display name, your avatar, that you train for the same goal, and your 0-100 training scores (consistency, progression and balance). Your recovery score, which is calculated from your Apple Health data, is shown only to you: it is never shown to other users and is not used in the scores they see. Your weight, height, sex, nutrition logs, Apple Health readings and progress photos are never shown to other users.

Links to other services

Some screens link to YouTube search results for exercise demonstrations. When you open one, YouTube (Google) receives your usual device information under its own policy.

Legal requests

We may disclose information when required by law (subpoena, court order) or to protect the safety of users or the public. We will push back on overbroad requests.

5. Data retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account from inside the app:

  • Your authentication record is permanently removed.
  • Your record of accepting our Terms and this policy is deleted, except for the minimal record described in section 2 (kept up to five years, without your name, email address or account ID).
  • Your workouts, posts, comments, likes, follows, push tokens, recovery readings, nutrition logs, and progress photos are permanently deleted (cascade delete).
  • Your avatar, post images, and progress photo files are deleted from storage.

Our hosting provider may keep automatic database backups for a limited period; deleted data is removed from backups as they expire.

What deleting your account does not do:

  • Subscription records. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, keeps purchase and subscription records linked to your Saura user ID under its own retention rules. Deleting your Saura account does not delete those records and does not cancel your subscription: cancel it first in your iPhone Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions. Apple keeps its own purchase records.
  • Information we may have to keep. We may keep limited information after deletion where the law requires it or to deal with a legal claim. For example, content reported as child sexual abuse material and related account details must be preserved and reported as the law requires.
  • Sign in with Apple. If you used Sign in with Apple, Saura asks Apple to disconnect it when you delete your account. You can also remove Saura yourself in your Apple Account settings under Sign in with Apple.

Some operational logs (sign-in IPs, server access logs) are retained by our infrastructure provider for up to 90 days for security and abuse-prevention purposes, after which they are automatically purged.

Providing data. Providing your email and password is required to create an account; everything else (display name, height, weight, photos, etc.) is optional. We do not use automated decision-making that produces legal effects.

8. Children's privacy

Saura is intended for users aged 16 and older. We enforce this with a date-of-birth check at sign-up, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If we learn that we have collected data from a user under 16, we will delete that data and terminate the account.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided data to us, please contact saura.app.help@gmail.com and we will act promptly.

9. Security

We use the following safeguards to protect your data:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit.
  • Access rules in our database (row-level security) that limit who can read and change each record. Content you choose to share, such as posts, can be read by the people who can view it.
  • A private storage bucket for progress photos, accessed only through short-lived signed links.
  • One-way password hashing: we never store plaintext passwords.
  • Email confirmation for accounts created with an email address.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will tell you without unreasonable delay and in any case within the time the law requires (for health information, no later than 60 days after we discover it), and we will notify the authorities where required.

10. International data transfers

Saura is operated from the United States. Our infrastructure providers (Supabase, Expo) may process data in the United States and other countries. See Supabase's and Expo's privacy policies for details.

Changes that do not reduce your rights or add new uses of your data, such as clarifications or typo fixes, take effect when posted. We keep a copy of each version we have published.
