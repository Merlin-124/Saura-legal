Common questions

Manage or cancel your subscription: Saura Pro is billed through your Apple ID. Manage or cancel anytime in Settings, your name, then Subscriptions on your iPhone.

Delete your account: Open Saura, then Settings, then Account, then Delete account. This permanently removes your account and the data described in section 5 of the Privacy Policy (for example, subscription records kept by our subscription provider are not removed). If you subscribe to Saura Pro, cancel it first in your iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions, because deleting your account does not stop Apple billing.

Notifications: Manage push and reminders in Saura Settings, or in iOS Settings, Notifications, Saura.

Apple Health: If you connect it, Saura reads data such as sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, steps, weight and nutrition to power your recovery score and trends (the full list is in the Privacy Policy). The Saura Apple Watch app also saves the workouts you finish on your watch to Apple Health. Manage access in iOS Settings, Privacy and Security, Health, Saura.

Report a problem or illegal content: In the app, tap the ••• menu on a post or comment and choose Report, or email saura.app.help@gmail.com with the subject "Report" and tell us where the content appears. For private intimate images shared without consent, see "Reporting illegal content" in our Terms of Use; you do not need an account.
