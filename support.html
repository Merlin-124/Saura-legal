Common questions

Manage or cancel your subscription: Saura Pro is billed through your Apple ID. Manage or cancel anytime in Settings, your name, then Subscriptions on your iPhone.

Delete your account: Open Saura, then Settings, then Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all associated data.

Notifications: Manage push and reminders in Saura Settings, or in iOS Settings, Notifications, Saura.

Apple Health: Saura can read heart rate, HRV, sleep, and steps to power your recovery score. Manage access in iOS Settings, Privacy and Security, Health, Saura.
